Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) & Data Specification

1. Core Stock Conservation Principle

Public health inventory auditing relies on mathematical stock conservation. For any reporting period, physical inventory movements must follow:

Calculated Ending Balance = Beginning Balance + Quantity Received − Quantity Dispensed − Losses & Adjustments

2. Supported Column Header Synonyms

The validator automatically identifies headers across varying eLMIS, DHIS2, and warehouse management software formats:

Internal Field Functional Definition Recognized Column Synonyms
Beginning Balance Opening inventory at period start Beginning Balance, Opening Balance, Start Balance, BB
Received Quantity received from stores or higher-level depots Received, Quantity Received, Receipts, Stock Received
Dispensed Quantity dispensed to patients/clients Dispensed, Quantity Dispensed, Consumption, Issued
Losses Adjustment Damages, expiries, inventory shrinkage, transfers Losses Adjustment, Losses and Adjustments, Losses, Adjustments
Ending Balance Physical count conducted at end of reporting cycle Ending Balance, Closing Balance, Physical Count, EB
Facility Name Reporting healthcare facility identifier Facility Name, Facility, Health Centre, Site
Facility Type Healthcare tier classification Facility Type, Classification, Type, Level
